# Demo dataset: Kestrel Group (fictitious)

Kestrel Group is a **fictitious** B2B SaaS company with three legal entities. Every name, customer, vendor,
FX rate and number here is synthetic, generated by [`generate.py`](generate.py) from a fixed seed, so the
same files come out every time.

| Entity | Country | Currency | Accounting system (export style) | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kestrel Inc. (parent) | US | USD | QuickBooks Online "Journal" report | `raw/us_kestrel_inc/Kestrel_Inc_Journal_Jan2025-Oct2026.csv` |
| Kestrel Ltd | UK | GBP | Xero "General Ledger Detail" | `raw/uk_kestrel_ltd/Kestrel_Ltd_General_Ledger_Detail.csv` |
| Kestrel GmbH | DE | EUR | DATEV "Buchungsstapel" (one file per fiscal year) | `raw/de_kestrel_gmbh/EXTF_Buchungsstapel_2025.csv`, `..._2026.csv` |

Also included:
- `raw/budget/FY2026_Budget_v3_FINAL.xlsx`: the board-approved budget, one sheet per entity, in local currency.
- `raw/fx/fx_rates_monthly.csv`: monthly average and closing rates to USD.

**Scale:** 22 months (Jan-2025 to Oct-2026), ~6,800 journals, ~14,000 ledger lines, about USD 2M monthly
revenue, 3 currencies and 73 local accounts (plus 2 new, unmapped ones) feeding a 27-account group chart.

## Why the files look the way they do

Each export copies the quirks of its source system. That is the realistic part of the work:

| System | Quirks the engine has to handle |
|---|---|
| QuickBooks style | 4 report-title lines before the header, a `TOTAL` footer row, account code and name in one field, sub-accounts (`Advertising:Search Ads`), `MM/DD/YYYY` dates |
| Xero style | UTF-8 with BOM, **day-first** `DD/MM/YYYY` dates (a month-first parser silently swaps 01/02 and 02/01), amounts formatted as `"1,234.56"` text, account codes with trailing spaces |
| DATEV style | Windows-1252 encoding, `;` separator, decimal comma, a metadata line before the header, `DDMM` dates **without the year** (the year sits in the metadata), and one row per booking with account and contra account plus a debit/credit flag (`S`/`H`) instead of double-entry lines |
| Budget workbook | Title rows, section headers, formula subtotals, a full-year column, wide months, FP&A's own line labels (`Advertising` instead of the ledger's `Advertising & Paid Media`) |

## Data-quality issues seeded on purpose

| # | Entity | What happened | What a manual close would miss | Caught by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | US | A USD 87.5k hosting bill appears twice in the Sep-2026 export | Gross margin shows 73.5% instead of 77.9%, and the trial balance still ties | `duplicate_lines` (quarantined automatically) |
| 2 | UK | The Oct-2026 payroll-tax journal was cut off by the export (one line missing) | GBP 13.9k of cost disappears | `unbalanced_journal` (blocking) |
| 3 | US | Two accounts opened in Oct-2026 (`6250 AI Tools & APIs`, `6720 Employer of Record Fees`) are not in the mapping | USD 27.7k drops out of the P&L | `unmapped_account` (blocking) |
| 4 | UK | R&D is tagged three ways (`Research and Development`, `R&D`, `Research & Dev`); the last one is not mapped | R&D looks USD 149k under budget in October when the cost is just mis-tagged | `unmapped_department` (warning) + commentary caution |
| 5 | DE | One travel booking lost its cost centre | Cost shows as unallocated | `missing_department` (warning) |
| 6 | US | (Business event, not a data error) a large customer churned in May-2026, hosting cost rose with new AI features from Aug-2026, and an unbudgeted conference took place in Jun-2026 | n/a: these are the variances the commentary should explain | variance commentary |

Regenerate with `python data/generate.py`.
